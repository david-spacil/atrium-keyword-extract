## Decision models over the controlled vocabulary (Option 4 of #2)

This is Option 4 of #2, as proposed there on 11-06. It is part of ufal/atrium-page-classification#58 (overview: ufal/atrium-project#NN). A decision model picks a term by walking down the vocabulary: facet, then group, then the terms' own tree, with "none of these" at every step. It covers the category and the meta-text decision only. Keywords stay as they are.

## Scope

1. **`dm_run.py`** (no torch) reads the same lines as the LLM runs, with the same functions: `llm_client_shared.read_input_rows()`, `should_process_line()`, the two-line context window, `excluded_prompt_themes()` and the geographic guardrail.
2. **The descent for each line:**
   - the steps are: meta-text (yes/no), then the facet, then the geographic gate (where the guardrail covers the branch), then the group, then the children;
   - at each level the children are offered, plus `<this term>` where the node is itself a term, plus `none_of_these`;
   - a beam keeps up to 3 children with p ≥ 0.2. The answer is the best path that ends in a term; if no path does, the line is unresolved;
   - the one node with more than 255 children (`historické oblasti a státní útvary`) is split into ordered chunks.
3. **The tree** is generated from `vocabulary_terms()` and `data_samples/vocab/specificity_pairs.csv` (`dm_run.py --dump-tree`). Its digest and the vocabulary version go into each run's paradata.
4. **Runs** on the 19 documents (1,029 lines kept by the quality filter):
   - **models:** Clef first, on Pavel's internal endpoint, then Clef-Flash and Strands Decider 2B;
   - **ablations:** beam 1 against beam 3; no context against two lines of context; guided descent (candidate terms ranked first).
5. **Output** in the shape of the LLM runs (`KW_PER_DOC_DM_<model>/`), plus a `decision` object: the path, the probabilities at each level, unresolved, lane, status, requests and latency.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `decision_client.py` is copied from page-classification and pinned in `tests/test_vendored_decision_parity.py`.
- [ ] The tree is generated, never written by hand, and its digest is recorded.
- [ ] The line-mode runs of each served model are scored with `score_categories.py` beside the R3 runs.
- [ ] `score_categories.py` counts a null category as unresolved, not as missing, with tests.
- [ ] Forced labels (the watch list of 10-16) are 0, or explained.
- [ ] Reported against R3: the unresolved share, the level where the descent stops, and the requests, latency and input tokens per line.
- [ ] The ablations are recorded.
- [ ] Each run records where the lines were sent, as agreed on ufal/atrium-project#NN.
- [ ] Once `gold_workbook.csv` has gold labels, these are posted on #2:
  - D1 scores and calibration per facet;
  - the D2 comparison with the constrained prompt and Option 3.

## Boundaries

- No change to the service, the prompt, `MODEL_KEY`, the defaults or the record during the pilot.
- No keyword (span) extraction.
- Facets and groups are used for routing only, never as a category (P1, P2).
- No hosted inference on archive text.
